Company Profile

S2 Global Inspection Group is an independent inspection, quality assurance, technical surveillance and expediting organisation supporting engineering, manufacturing, fabrication, construction and industrial supply chains.

Read full profile

We provide objective verification against agreed specifications, drawings, inspection plans, purchase orders, codes, standards and contractual requirements. Our focus is technical competence, impartiality, integrity, safety, confidentiality and sustainable project outcomes.

Our service philosophy is: Inspect with integrity. Verify with evidence. Deliver sustainable solutions.

General Terms & Conditions

The contractual framework defines the agreed scope, client responsibilities, inspection limitations, reporting, confidentiality, fees, claims and liability allocation.

Read key terms

Client responsibility: The Client remains responsible for design, specifications, statutory approvals, information supplied, safe site conditions, final commercial/technical decisions and the obligations of manufacturers, contractors and suppliers.

Inspection limitation: S2 reports observations and verification within the agreed scope at the time and place of inspection. Inspection does not constitute a design approval, product warranty, guarantee of future performance or fitness-for-purpose determination unless expressly contracted.

Third-party responsibility: Inspection does not transfer manufacturer, supplier, contractor, designer or operator responsibilities to S2.

Liability: Subject to applicable law and the agreed contract, S2's liability is limited to the professional fees paid for the specific service giving rise to the claim, excluding liabilities that cannot lawfully be excluded or limited.

Important: The full executed quotation/service agreement and applicable law govern the final contractual position.
